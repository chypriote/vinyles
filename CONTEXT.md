# Vinyles

Nico's record collection and wishlist, shared with family.

## Language

### Collection

**Record**:
One physical vinyl copy of an Album that Nico owns, in one Variant.
_Avoid_: Vinyl, entry, item, copy

**Album**:
One work by one Artist, with a title and a release year. Records and Wishlist Items are of an Album.
_Avoid_: Release, title, LP

**Cover**:
The front image of an Album. All Records and Wishlist Items of the Album show it.
_Avoid_: Artwork, picture, sleeve

**Variant**:
What makes a pressing of an album different from other pressings: color, signature, edition.
_Avoid_: Version, edition, color

**Artist**:
The band or musician that made an album. Records and wishlist items belong to one artist.
_Avoid_: Band, artiste, group

**Collection**:
All Records that Nico owns.

**Acquisition**:
How a Record came to Nico: a Purchase or a Gift, with a date.

**Purchase**:
An Acquisition where Nico paid a price, in euros, at a Store.
_Avoid_: Order, buy

**Gift**:
An Acquisition where someone gave Nico the Record. It has no store and no price, and can name the giver.
_Avoid_: Cadeau, present

**Store**:
A shop where Nico buys records or finds Price Quotes. "Official Store" is one Store: the shop of whichever artist it is.
_Avoid_: Shop, vendor, "Acheté sur"

### People

**Owner**:
Nico, the only person who changes the Collection and the Wishlist. The Owner logs in with a password.

**Viewer**:
A family member who looks at the Collection and the Wishlist and changes nothing.
_Avoid_: User, guest

### Wishlist

**Wishlist**:
All albums that Nico wants and does not own yet.
_Avoid_: WL

**Wishlist Item**:
An Album that Nico wants, maybe in a given Variant, with a Desire.

**Desire**:
How much Nico wants a Wishlist Item, from 1 to 3, where 3 is the most.
_Avoid_: Priority, rating

**Price Quote**:
The price, in euros, that one Store asks for a Wishlist Item.

**Got it**:
Nico gets a Wishlist Item: it leaves the Wishlist and becomes a Record.
_Avoid_: Buy, convert, move

**Drop**:
Nico no longer wants a Wishlist Item: it leaves the Wishlist and does not become a Record.
_Avoid_: Delete, remove
